Who We Are

More Than a Government-
A Movement

The SUG SUMAS is the democratically elected body that represents every student of the State University of Medical and Applied Sciences,Igbo-Eno.

🎓 Our Mission To protect, promote, and advance the academic and social welfare of all SUMASites through democratic governance.
🌍 Our Vision A united, empowered, and globally competitive student body that drives Welfare,innovation, leadership, and community growth.
⚖️ Our Values Integrity · Inclusivity · Transparency · Service · Excellence

Founded on the principles of democratic representation, the Student's Union Government at SUMAS serves as the official bridge between the student body and university administration.

The executive, House of Senate, House of Representative, ministers, and special assistants work tirelessly across multiple departments — from science and technology to welfare, infrastructure, and social development — ensuring no student is left behind.

We organize events, process student complaints, champion academic freedom, and drive campus innovation through dedicated ministries and special offices.
